## Market Gap Analysis

### What the Existing 5 Books Cover:
| Book | Title | Coverage |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1 | From Zero | Absolute beginner foundation |
| 2 | Complete Guide | Comprehensive beginner→intermediate (pronunciation, daily life, travel, business basics, living in China) |
| 3 | Real Lessons | Authentic classroom content (daily basics, travel stories, culture, business modern, society) |
| 4 | Survival Chinese | Pocket phrasebook (essentials, eating, transport, money, emergency) |
| 5 | Business Chinese | Full professional immersion (workplace, relationships, deals, career) |

### What's NOT Covered (Identified Gaps):
1. **Chinese characters & reading/writing** — The #1 barrier for learners, zero coverage
2. **Food & cuisine culture in depth** — Only surface-level restaurant ordering
3. **China's digital ecosystem** — Only 2 chapters (Book 2 ch22, Book 3 ch15) in 100+ chapters total
4. **Story-based learning** — No narrative/storytelling approach; graded readers dominate Amazon
5. **Social life, relationships & family** — Dating, friendship, family dynamics completely untouched

### 2026 Market Trends Supporting These Choices:
- Character/writing apps (Skritter) are top-rated tools
- Food content drives cultural engagement (#1 entry point to Chinese culture)
- "Digital literacy for China" is a top trending topic
- Graded readers (Mandarin Companion, Chinese Breeze) are Amazon bestsellers
- "Chinese culture & society" and "cross-cultural topics" are growing fast
- 76% of foreign Chinese learners are under 30 → digital & social content resonates

## Book 6: Z Turns Chinese — Unlock Chinese Characters
### 解码汉字：外国人看得懂的汉字指南

**Full Title:** Z Turns Chinese: Unlock Chinese Characters — The Visual Guide to Reading & Writing Chinese
**Subtitle:** See the Logic Behind Every Character — From Radicals to Reading
**Price Point:** $12.99-16.99 (reference/workbook)
**Color:** (30, 100, 180) — Deep Blue

### Design Philosophy

Every Chinese learner hits the character wall. Books 1-5 teach speaking and listening, but characters remain a black box. Book 6 cracks it open — showing that Chinese characters are NOT random drawings but a **logical visual system** with patterns, stories, and rules.

**Core Principle:** Characters tell stories. Every stroke has a reason. Once you see the system, thousands of characters become learnable.

**Inspired by:** GetNote recordings where students ask "How do I read this?", "Why does this character look like that?", Tony's explanations of character etymology in class.

### Target Audience
- Learners who can speak some Chinese but can't read (Books 1-3 graduates)
- Visual learners who need a different approach
- HSK prep students (HSK requires character recognition)
- Anyone who wants to read menus, signs, and WeChat messages

## Book 7: Z Turns Chinese — Eat Your Way Through China
### 吃遍中国：用美食学中文

**Full Title:** Z Turns Chinese: Eat Your Way Through China — Learn Chinese Through the World's Greatest Food Culture
**Subtitle:** Order Like a Local, Cook Like a Chef, and Understand China Through Its Food
**Price Point:** $12.99-16.99 (lifestyle + language)
**Color:** (200, 50, 30) — Appetizing Red

### Design Philosophy

Food is the #1 gateway to Chinese culture. Every foreigner's best Chinese conversations happen at the dinner table. Book 7 turns this into a complete learning experience — teaching Chinese through China's incredible regional cuisines, cooking culture, and food philosophy.

**Core Principle:** You don't learn a language in a classroom. You learn it at a 大排档 (street food stall) at midnight, arguing about whether 麻辣 or 酸辣 is better.

**Inspired by:** The dozens of GetNote recordings where food dominates the conversation — Tony and students discussing 火锅, 街头美食, 饮食文化, tea culture, and regional cuisines.

### Target Audience
- Food lovers who want to explore Chinese cuisine deeper
- Travelers who want to eat beyond tourist restaurants
- Home cooks interested in Chinese cooking vocabulary
- Anyone who bonds over food (i.e., everyone)

China's digital ecosystem is unlike anywhere else on Earth. You can't use Google, Facebook, or WhatsApp. Instead, you live inside WeChat, pay with Alipay, shop on Taobao, hail rides on Didi, and order food on Meituan. Book 8 teaches you the Chinese you need to thrive in this digital world.

**Core Principle:** In 2026 China, your phone IS your life. If you can read your phone screen in Chinese, you can do anything.
