## How This Book Is Different

Every sentence in this book uses our **Four-Layer Translation System**:

```
Layer 1:  你    好     吗？           Chinese Characters
Layer 2:  nǐ    hǎo    ma?            Pinyin (with tones)
Layer 3:  you   good   [question]?    Word-by-Word (Chinese word order)
Layer 4:  "How are you?"              Natural English
```

This lets you see exactly how Chinese thinks — not just what it says.
